Inspired by the documentary film *Life Matters* (2019) and clinical research into pediatric oncology wards in Chinese public hospitals, **Your virtual buddy: UU** explores how extended reality (XR) can support children aged 6–12 undergoing long-term inpatient chemotherapy for acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL).

Beyond the physical toll of lumbar punctures, bone marrow aspirations, and multi-hour intravenous infusions, hospitalized children experience prolonged isolation in sterile wards, separation from peers, and a profound loss of agency over their daily environment.

---

## Clinical Journey Mapping & Spatial UI Design System

Through clinical literature review and end-to-end journey mapping of pediatric oncology wards, we identified three high-stress touchpoints across a child's treatment cycle:

1. **Ward Admission & Disorientation**: Entering an unfamiliar clinical environment stripped of personal comfort or play.
2. **Procedural Anxiety & Infusion Routines**: Enduring repetitive, painful procedures and hours of restricted mobility during IV drips.
3. **Immunocompromised Isolation**: Weeks of recovery inside sterile rooms with minimal social contact.

To address these moments without causing motion sickness or cognitive overload for fatigued patients, we designed the virtual companion character **UU** and authored a **pediatric spatial UI design system** featuring low-fatigue gaze and single-hand interactions, comforting color palettes, and seated/bedside ergonomic viewing zones.

---

## Unity VR Prototype & Stanford Meaningful XR 2024 Presentation

- **PICO DevJam 2023 Prototype**: Engineered a playable standalone VR prototype in **Unity (C#)** using the **PICO XR SDK**, allowing young patients to customize their bedside space, care for and converse with **UU**, and practice guided breathing and distraction exercises during procedures.
- **Academic & Industry Recognition**: Following the hackathon prototype, the research and spatial interaction framework were selected and presented at **Meaningful XR 2024** at **Stanford University** (May 2024).
