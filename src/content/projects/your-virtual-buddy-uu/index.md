Inspired by the documentary film *Life Matters* (2019), *Your virtual buddy: UU* explores how extended reality can support pediatric leukemia patients undergoing long-term hospitalization. Beyond the physical toll of chemotherapy, hospitalized children experience prolonged isolation, fear of clinical procedures, and a loss of agency over their daily environment.

## Research & XR Prototype

Through clinical literature review and user journey mapping of pediatric oncology wards in Chinese public hospitals, we identified key emotional friction points across admission, daily infusion routines, and isolation recovery.

To address these moments, we designed the virtual companion character **UU**, built a cohesive spatial UI design system tailored for young patients, and implemented a playable VR prototype in Unity for PICO headsets during the 2023 PICO DevJam hackathon. The project was subsequently selected and presented at **Meaningful XR 2024** at Stanford University.
