**Water Break Timer** is a playful browser utility that pairs a custom webcam image classifier with the **Web Notifications API** to enforce regular hydration breaks during long coding sessions.

## How It Works

Instead of a passive timer that can be dismissed with a click, the app watches the webcam feed to verify whether you actually took a sip:

- **Idle Detection (`Just me :(`)**: While sitting at the desk without a cup in frame, the countdown bar ticks down toward `0:00`.
- **System Alert (`Time's up!`)**: When the timer hits `0:00`, the status bar turns red and dispatches a native macOS browser notification: *"Water Break Timer — Time's up! Take a water break."*
- **Mug Verification (`Mug`)**: Raising a mug to take a drink switches the classifier label to `Mug` and automatically resets the countdown timer.
