# Project P.K.P



![project-pkp image 1](/assets/projects/project-pkp/project-pkp-1.jpg)

https://pkp.katudio.com/

The Panda in the U.S. incident, which was the subject of a social media frenzy in China last year, is the starting point for this work. In addition to the printed and hand-drawn work, there is also a game for the online experience. The main idea of the work is that a panda who knows nothing can be a political weapon to harm people.
