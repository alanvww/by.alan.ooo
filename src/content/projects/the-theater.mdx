# The Theater



![the-theater image 1](/assets/projects/the-theater/the-theater-1.jpg)

A VR interactive theatre play project, blends traditional Chinese theatre aesthetics with modern machinery design, showcasing the mix of modern peroformance and advanced mocap technology and experimental VR interactive methods, to explore themes of individualism and collectivism.

The Theater is an immersive VR experience that bridges performance and interaction through advanced AI motion capture and finger-tracking technology, allowing controller-free engagement. The work presents two contrasting virtual worlds: one embodying Eastern collectivism and the other Western individualism. Each world unfolds as a living stage, offering performances that players can actively participate in, shaping their experiences through movement and gesture. By immersing the audience in these dual narratives, the piece invites reflection on the interplay between personal identity and communal belonging, exploring how these cultural philosophies influence our roles and relationships in society.

The accompanying 3D short film, titled "Shan Gui", serves as a visual and thematic showcase for the VR project. This film reimagines the Mountain Spirit from Qu Yuans poem as an androgynous figure, merging traditional Chinese theatre visuals with modern machinery design. Created using Unreal Engine 5, the short film highlights our artistic and technical vision, demonstrating the potential of digital animation and interactive storytelling. It also introduces viewers to the narratives core themes of isolation, longing, and cultural influence.

Players experience mesmerizing performances blending AI motion capture (Qian Mian) and OptiTrack, capturing the artistry of professional dancers. AI mocap ensures dancers can perform freely in their most comfortable space, while OptiTrack provides high-precision tracking for multi-performer interactions, creating a dynamic and immersive choreography.

AI-generated 3D modeling technology is also incorporated to create intricate, avant-garde ornaments and accessories, pushing the boundaries of digital fashion design.
